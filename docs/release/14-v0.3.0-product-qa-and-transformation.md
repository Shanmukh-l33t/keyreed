## 1. Executive Summary

Following the dual-persona Master Directive (**Persona 1: Ordinary Everyday Customer** and **Persona 2: Principal Product Engineer**), we started by downloading the published `v0.2.0` `KeyReed-Setup-x64.exe` from GitHub, backing up `%LOCALAPPDATA%\keyreed` to simulate a brand-new user on a clean Windows 11 desktop, installing the package, and launching it from the Windows Start Menu shortcut (`KeyReed.lnk`).

Across **7 iterative testing and engineering rounds**, we discovered **20 concrete customer-facing defects and edge cases (`DEF-01` through `DEF-020`)**, root-caused and fixed every single one, downloaded and verified **all 6 ASR models** on Windows 11, validated **all 6 writing modes** and **all 3 output modes** (`Auto Insert`, `Clipboard Only`, `Ask Before Inserting` with `Yes` / `No` / `Cancel`), ran **30 consecutive short dictations** and **20 consecutive long dictations**, executed adversarial fault-injection tests, and verified the packaged `v0.3.0` installer upgrade, uninstallation, and fresh installation flows.

## 7. Remaining Limitations

1. **Unsigned Windows Binaries**: `KeyReed-Setup-x64.exe` and `keyreed.exe` are not Authenticode-signed with an EV/OV certificate yet, so Windows SmartScreen may prompt on initial download until the user verifies `SHA256SUMS.txt`.
2. **Physical Human Acoustic Diversity**: While WASAPI hardware capture was verified on the physical `OWL 4K PRO Audio` and `Realtek(R) Audio` microphones and speech transcription was tested across 12 diverse audio recordings (including fast, slow, quiet, noisy, technical, and Indian English phrasing), live acoustic speech from multiple physical human speakers in the room was not performed during this unattended run.
3. **Windows 10 Regression Pass**: All live desktop verification was executed on Windows 11 x64 (`Build 26200`); Windows 10 x64 shares the same Win32/WASAPI APIs but was not separately booted during this session.

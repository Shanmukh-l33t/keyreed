# KeyReed 2.0 release notes (draft)

Draft by lane B from plan sections C1 to C3. Every feature below is marked "to be confirmed by the main builder": delete what did not ship, and replace each [bracket] with a measured figure or remove the sentence. Nothing here has been checked against a build.

KeyReed 2.0 adds three things beside dictation: fixing the text you select, a small writing window, and a memory for your own words. Dictation is unchanged.

## Privacy

What you say, write and correct stays on this PC. KeyReed never logs keystrokes and never captures the screen. To learn your words it re-checks the text box it just typed into, for up to a minute, compares in memory and keeps only the corrected words. One switch turns that off.

*To be confirmed by the main builder: this sentence replaces nothing; it is added to the website and README beside the existing network, audio and clipboard promises. Keep it only if the learning feature shipped as described.*

## Quick Fix

*To be confirmed by the main builder.*

Select some text in almost any app, press the Quick Fix shortcut [default to be confirmed], and KeyReed corrects typos, missing apostrophes, doubled words and common slips. It fixes; it does not rewrite. Your wording stays yours.

- **You see the changes before anything is replaced.** The Review panel shows each changed word; Enter replaces the selection, Esc leaves it alone. Switching on silent replacement is a setting, and applies only to kinds of fix that passed KeyReed's own accuracy test [list the kinds that shipped].
- **Undo** is on the pill for a few seconds after a fix.
- **Deeper** asks a language model for a second, closer look and shows the result in the Review panel. It never replaces text by itself. [Say whether it runs on this PC or needs the cloud switch.]
- **Works offline.** The grammar model [name, size 112 MB] is a download on first use and runs on this PC. [Typical time per paragraph on a CPU-only laptop.]
- **Protects what it should not touch**: links, e-mail addresses, file paths, numbers, names, code and shell commands are left exactly as typed.
- **English only** for now.

What it does not do: it does not work in administrator windows, password fields or terminals, and it does not keep the formatting inside a selection that has links, tables or lists (it tells you and opens Review instead).

How your clipboard is used: to read your selection KeyReed makes a copy through the Windows clipboard, so that copy can appear in Windows clipboard history and in any clipboard manager you use. KeyReed saves what was on your clipboard first and puts it back afterwards. If your clipboard holds something KeyReed cannot put back, it stops before copying and says so. KeyReed's own clipboard writes are marked to stay out of history and cloud sync.

## The Composer

*To be confirmed by the main builder.*

Click the KeyReed pill and a small writing window opens beside your text. If you had text selected, a chip says "Using selection, 412 characters from Outlook" and you can remove it.

- **Six actions**: Fix, Improve, Shorter, Change tone, Summarise, and your own instruction. Follow-ups work on the latest result.
- **Streaming and cancel**: the answer appears as it is written; Esc cancels.
- **Insert, Replace, Copy, Regenerate** once you have a result. If KeyReed cannot paste where you were, the result stays on the clipboard and says so.
- **Speak your instruction**: the dictation key works inside the window, and a voice-command shortcut applies a spoken instruction to your selection.
- **Always shows where it runs**: "On this PC, model name" or "Cloud, host, model". Nothing is sent anywhere before you press Submit.
- **Better local models, optional**: Qwen3.5 2B (about 1.3 GB) and 4B (about 2.7 GB), both Apache-2.0, as downloads you choose; the bundled small model is used until you pick one [say so in the window]. [Which one is suggested for which PC.]
- **Cloud is a separate switch**, off by default. Agreeing to cloud dictation does not send a selected email anywhere.

**Changed: clicking the pill.** Clicking the pill used to start and stop dictation. It now opens the Composer. A new setting, "When I click the pill", can be Open the Composer, Start dictation or Do nothing. If you had the old click turned off it stays off. Dictation by shortcut, and "Start dictation" in the pill's right-click menu, are unchanged.

## My style

*To be confirmed by the main builder.*

KeyReed learns the words and habits that make your writing yours, and keeps a short list you can read and edit.

- **Your words.** When you correct a word KeyReed typed, for example "cooper netties" to "Kubernetes", KeyReed notices. The second time it sees the same correction it adds the word to your dictionary and says so once on the pill, with Undo. A correction seen once waits in a Suggestions list.
- **Where it looks.** Only the one text box KeyReed just typed into, for up to a minute, in apps that share their text with Windows [list the apps checked]. Chrome, Edge and other browser-based apps are not read in 2.0. Password fields, terminals, password managers, private-browsing windows, administrator windows and any app on your exclusion list are never read.
- **Your habits.** From text you hand to Fix or Improve, and from samples you paste into Settings, KeyReed counts spelling style (British or American), contractions, sentence length, serial commas, dash style, full stops on short messages and emoji. It keeps the counts, not the text.
- **Your choices.** KeyReed counts which actions and suggestions you accept or reject.
- **The Style Card.** Each habit is listed with its evidence ("British spelling, 41 of 44 sentences") and a scope (everywhere, Email, Chat or Documents). A habit applies only after at least 30 sentences with about 80 percent agreement [starting values; confirm]. Keep, Edit or Remove any item; anything you set yourself is never overridden.
- **How it is used.** Quick Fix leaves alone habits you hold, and uses your spelling style. Writing requests in the Composer get a short style note. Learned words also help the speech recogniser and the dictation clean-up, as the dictionary already does.
- **Controls.** One switch turns learning from edits off; Pause, Reset, Export and Delete everything are in Settings. Learned data lives in `style.json` beside your history file, as plain readable JSON, and is never sent anywhere.

What it never learns: text you selected for Summarise, Explain or Reply (someone else's writing), anything outside the box KeyReed typed into, audio, anything while learning is paused.

Honest Comparison

KeyReed vs Wispr Flow.

Both let you hold a key, speak, and get clean text in the app you are using. The difference is where your voice goes, what you pay, and which devices you need it on.

Choose KeyReed if

  • you want your voice processed on your own PC, with dictation that keeps working offline
  • you dictate on Windows and do not want a word limit, a subscription, or an account
  • you want to read the Rust source, choose from eight SHA-256-pinned speech models, or build it yourself
  • you dictate code, commands, and file names (Technical and Terminal / Code modes) or want local Quick Fix & Composer
Download KeyReed

Choose Wispr Flow if

  • you also need synced dictation on a Mac, iPhone, or Android
  • you dictate in languages beyond KeyReed's models (Flow lists 100+)
  • you want to edit selected text by voice (Command Mode), voice snippets, or a meeting Notetaker
  • your company requires SSO, centralized admin controls, or SOC 2 Type II, ISO 27001, and HIPAA BAA paperwork

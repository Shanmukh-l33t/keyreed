Comparison

KeyReed vs Wispr Flow

Both let you hold a key, speak, and get clean text in the app you are using. The difference is where your voice goes, what you pay, and which devices you need it on.

Wispr Flow details are from its own website and help centre, checked on 17 November 2026. KeyReed is an independent project, not affiliated with Wispr.

Choose KeyReed if

  • you want your voice processed on your own PC, and dictation that keeps working offline
  • you dictate on Windows and do not want a word limit, a subscription or an account
  • you want to read the code, pick the speech model, or build it yourself
  • you dictate code, commands and file names (Technical and Terminal / Code modes)
Download KeyReed

Choose Wispr Flow if

  • you also need dictation on a Mac, iPhone or Android
  • you dictate in languages beyond KeyReed's models (Flow lists 100+)
  • you want to edit selected text by voice, snippets, or a meeting notetaker
  • your company needs SSO, admin controls or HIPAA and SOC 2 paperwork

What "local" means in KeyReed

In the default profile, audio is captured into memory while you hold the shortcut, transcribed by a speech model on your PC, cleaned up by rules and an optional local grammar model, and typed into your app. On the normal path it is never written to a file, and no network call is made for audio or text.

The network is used for one thing by default: the speech model download you start during setup. Two optional profiles can send audio or text to a cloud provider, and only after you enter your own API key. The source is public, so you can check all of this yourself.

Where Wispr Flow is ahead

Flow is a mature, funded product. It runs on four platforms, supports far more languages, edits text by voice with Command Mode, and offers snippets, a notetaker and team administration with security certifications.

KeyReed is a young open-source project for Windows. Its builds are not code-signed yet, and its speed and accuracy figures come from one test laptop. If any of Flow's strengths are what you need, Flow is the better choice.
